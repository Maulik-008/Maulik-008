## About

I build intelligent, scalable software by combining full-stack development with AI-powered solutions. My core stack is Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL, React.js, Next.js, TypeScript, and Go, and I integrate AI into that stack to solve real business problems rather than bolt it on as an afterthought.

My focus is AI-driven products: LLM-powered applications, AI workflows, intelligent automation, RAG systems, and AI agents that connect cleanly into the platforms a business already uses. Most of that work turns a manual, multi-step business process into something automated and easy for a non-technical team to run.

That experience spans SaaS platforms, healthcare technology, telemedicine, patient management systems, and enterprise applications, where security, scalability, and uptime aren't optional.
